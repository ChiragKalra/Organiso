Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 October 2026

Organiso is a free, open source SMS organizer developed by Chirag Kalra. This policy explains the information the app uses, your backup choices, and the third-party services involved.

Your messages are organized on your device. SMS backups are optional. You choose whether to save them on your device or authorize Google Drive backups. Automatic backups are off by default.

Information used by the app

Organiso accesses the following information, with Android permissions, to provide its messaging features:

  • SMS messages: message text, sender and recipient addresses, timestamps, message status, and related metadata are processed locally to display conversations, categorize messages, and send or receive SMS.
  • Phone and SIM information: phone state, phone numbers where available, and SIM information are used on your device for messaging and dual-SIM preferences.
  • Contacts: your address book is read locally to display saved contact names alongside phone numbers.

Phone numbers, contacts, and SMS data are used locally for SMS organization. Optional backups transfer SMS data only to the destination you choose, as described below. Organiso does not sell your SMS or contact data.

Optional SMS backups

Backups contain SMS text and the metadata supplied by Android, including sender and recipient addresses, timestamps, message status, and SIM metadata. They do not include MMS attachments, your contact address book, Organiso settings, or its classification database. Creating a backup reads your SMS without deleting or changing messages.

Back up to this device

A manual backup saves a JSON file to a location you choose through Android’s file picker. Automatic device backups save files in a folder you select; Organiso retains permission to access that folder across app and device restarts.

Backup files are not encrypted by Organiso. Anyone with access to the saved file can read the messages it contains. Choose a location you trust and manage access to any copies you make.

Back up to Google Drive

Google Drive backup requires you to choose a Google account and authorize access to Organiso’s private app-data folder using the drive.appdata scope. This permission does not grant Organiso access to your other Google Drive files.

When you request a Drive backup or enable its automatic schedule, a copy of your SMS backup is uploaded to that account over an encrypted HTTPS connection. The JSON file is not separately encrypted by Organiso. These backups are not uploaded to the developer’s servers.

The selected Google account name is stored locally to identify the backup account. Google Play services manages authorization tokens; Organiso does not store access tokens in its preferences.

Automatic backups and your controls

Device and Google Drive schedules are separate and off by default. You can independently choose daily, weekly, or monthly backups for either destination, change the selected folder, or turn a schedule off. Android may defer work based on battery, storage, and network conditions. Drive backups can be set to wait for an unmetered connection.

Drive schedules use your existing Google authorization without opening sign-in or consent screens in the background. If authorization needs attention, you must reconnect from the app.

Turning a schedule off stops future scheduled backups without removing earlier files. Disconnecting Google Drive revokes Organiso’s access and disables its Drive schedule; Organiso does not delete existing Drive backups when you disconnect.

Storage, retention, and deletion

Data used for SMS organization is stored on your device. Temporary backup files created inside the app are removed after the backup operation finishes or fails.

Saved device backups remain in the location you selected until you delete them. Drive backups remain subject to your Google account and Drive settings. The app-data folder is hidden from the ordinary Drive file list; you can manage or delete Organiso’s hidden app data through Google Drive’s app management settings. Removing that app data can delete the backups it contains.

Disabling backups or uninstalling Organiso should not be treated as deleting copies you previously saved or shared. For questions about information you submitted directly to the developer, including a request to delete a bug report or attachment, contact the address below.

Bug reports

When you choose to submit a bug report, the report title, description, and any image you attach are sent to the developer through Firebase Realtime Database and Firebase Storage. The report also includes your Android version, device manufacturer, and model to help diagnose the problem.

Information you include in a description or attachment is part of that submission. Avoid including private message contents or other personal details unless you intend to share them with the developer.

Analytics, diagnostics, and service providers

Organiso uses Google Play services for Google account authorization and Firebase services for app analytics, performance diagnostics, and submitted bug reports. These services may process information such as app usage events, device and app information, identifiers, IP addresses, and performance or diagnostic data under their own policies.

Analytics events generated by Organiso describe feature usage and do not include SMS text, phone numbers, or contact names. Message backups are handled separately, using the destination you selected.

Read the relevant providers’ privacy information:

  • Google Privacy Policy, including Google Play services and Google Drive
  • Google Analytics for Firebase
  • Firebase privacy and security information

Security

Organiso uses Android permissions and private app storage for its internal data, and HTTPS for Google Drive backup transfers. Exported JSON backups are not encrypted by the app. No method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed completely secure.

Links to other sites

The app and this policy may link to third-party sites. Their operators control their content and data practices. Please review their privacy policies when you use those services.

Children’s privacy

The data practices described in this policy apply to all users. Parents or guardians with questions about a child’s information can contact the developer using the address below.
