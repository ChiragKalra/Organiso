About Organiso

Organiso is an innovative new messaging app which allows you to lay back and let the app work for you. Through its predictive technology, Organiso automatically organises your inbox for you, while also offering a plethora of useful features.

Comes with a simplistic UI.

Privacy Policy

We value your privacy.

Organiso organizes your messages on your device, with zero advertisements. SMS backups are optional: save them on your device or authorize Google Drive backups, with separate automatic schedules for each destination.
